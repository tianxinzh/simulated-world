Every device along the front edge of the table is physically interactive. Drag the red throttle lever up or down; buttons depress when clicked; toggle switches flip between modes. The same actions are mirrored here for keyboard and touch users.

Drag empty space to orbit, scroll to zoom, and hold Shift while dragging to pan. Use ↑ / ↓ to change speed, 0 to return to the front-of-table view, C to hide the UI, and Esc to close this panel.

This diorama is an artistic compression of real San Francisco and Bay Area elements, not a literal rail map. Fact-checked references include the Golden Gate Bridge in International Orange, the Ferry Building clock tower, the Transamerica Pyramid, Coit Tower on Telegraph Hill, the Painted Ladies at Alamo Square, Muni historic streetcars, San Francisco cable cars, Alcatraz Island, and Port of Oakland container cranes.
