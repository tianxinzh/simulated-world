M toggles synthesized ambience; H plays a terminal chime. Sound starts only after your interaction. The top-right EN / 中 button changes the interface and console labels.

A 240-second day / night cycle. Space pauses traffic, not the sky. This airport is an artistic Bay Area miniature, not a replica of SFO or a map to scale. Geometry, wood grain, labels, lights, and audio are generated inside this file. Only Three.js is loaded from a CDN.
